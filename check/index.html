What gets checked

Every answer comes from ArcFlagRegistry, a contract on Arc mainnet. It was built from an index of every Uniswap V4 swap on Arc: 4.59 million swaps across 200,163 pools. It records two facts today.

Uses the USDC name at another address
USDC is Arc's gas token and lives at one address, 0x3600…0000. Any other contract calling itself USDC gets recorded, because buying it doesn't get you dollars.
Pool keeps 50% or more of a trade
Measured from the fee actually taken on real swaps, not the pool's stated setting. A hook can override that setting, and several on Arc do.
