What every swap on Arc shows

We indexed every Uniswap V4 swap on Arc straight from its public RPC, with no paid indexer in between. Two things a buyer should know before trading stood out, and both are now recorded on-chain.

Tokens posing as USDC

On Arc, USDC pays for gas and lives at one address. Contracts that call themselves USDC at any other address still trade, and some of them are among the busiest tokens on the chain.

    Limits, stated plainly

    • Flags are facts about chain history, not verdicts on the people behind a token.
    • One address publishes flags today. setPublisher is where that opens up; it hasn't yet.
    • Clearing a flag costs the same as setting one, so a mistake is cheap to fix. Report one on GitHub.
    • Records hold through the block shown on each one. Newer pools aren't in until the next publishing round.
